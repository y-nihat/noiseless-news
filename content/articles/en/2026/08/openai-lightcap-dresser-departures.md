## What happened

Brad Lightcap, who had been OpenAI's chief operating officer since 2022 after
joining the company in 2018, announced on 11 August 2026 that he is leaving
OpenAI entirely [2][3][4]. He had already stepped back from the COO title in
an April 2026 executive reorganization, moving into a special-projects role
reporting directly to CEO Sam Altman; his August announcement is a full
departure from the company, not a title change. In his own post, Lightcap
described the move as "bittersweet" and said he is leaving to "start
something new," citing "a few important new things the world will need to
get right" as reasons for the shift, without giving specifics [3].

Two days later, on 13 August 2026, OpenAI confirmed that Chief Revenue
Officer Denise Dresser -- who had joined the company in December 2025 after
more than a decade at Salesforce, including a stint as Slack's CEO -- is also
leaving, to pursue other opportunities [1][5]. OpenAI named Dali Rajic, most
recently president and COO of Wiz (acquired by Google), as her successor,
in a post on its own site [1][6][7].

Neither departure came with an on-the-record OpenAI statement explaining the
reasons; both were announced through the executives' own posts, with
OpenAI's confirmation of Dresser's exit arriving alongside the announcement
of her replacement rather than as a separate statement on her departure
itself.

## Why it matters

Both exits follow Lightcap's own move out of the COO role during an April
2026 executive reorganization, part of a broader pattern of short tenures and
turnover among OpenAI's senior leadership this year. Losing a revenue chief
eight months into the job, as OpenAI works to grow its enterprise business,
adds to that pattern.
