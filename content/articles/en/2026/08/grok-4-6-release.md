## What happened

SpaceXAI — xAI's brand since its July 2026 rebrand under SpaceX — released Grok
4.6 on 12 August 2026 [1]. The high-reasoning tier is priced at $2 per million
input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, with a $0.50-per-million
cache-hit rate; a separate "fast" variant costs twice as much [1][2].

Artificial Analysis, an independent benchmark tracker, fetched live at
publication time, scores Grok 4.6 at 61 on its Intelligence Index — a score it
describes as "effectively the same" as OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, also at 61, and one
point ahead of Kimi K3's 60 [2][3]. Both remain behind Anthropic's current
lineup: Claude Opus 5 (63) and Claude Fable 5 (62) [2][3]. Coverage of the
release was broad, but nearly all of it traces back to just these two origins —
xAI's own announcement and Artificial Analysis's benchmark — rather than
separate independent reporting.

## Why it matters

Grok 4.5, released in July, positioned itself on price and efficiency while
trailing Anthropic's top models on capability. Grok 4.6 narrows that gap on an
independent index — landing in a near-tie with OpenAI's flagship rather than
clearly behind it — while keeping the same aggressive pricing. Anthropic's
Opus 5 and Fable 5 still lead the tracked field.
