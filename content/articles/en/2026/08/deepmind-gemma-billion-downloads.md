## What happened

Google DeepMind said on 20 August 2026 that its Gemma family of open-weight
models has passed one billion cumulative downloads, and that outside
developers have published more than 100,000 Gemma model variants over the
past two years [1]. The figures come from a post co-authored by Clement
Farabet, Google DeepMind's Vice President, and Olivier Lacombe, its Product
Director.

As one named example, India's National Health Authority integrated Gemma
models and Google's open Medical Data Toolkit into Aarogya Setu 2.0, a
government health app with more than 100 million Android downloads, to
convert medical reports into standardized digital formats [1].

## Why it matters

A billion downloads and 100,000-plus published variants is Google's own
measure of how widely an open-weight model family gets reused and adapted
once released, rather than only run through the vendor's own products. The
Aarogya Setu 2.0 integration is a concrete, named case of that reuse at
national scale, rather than a hypothetical.
