## What happened

Marvell disclosed on 19 August 2026 that it has granted Google a warrant to
buy up to 58,970,907 of its shares at $206.58 each -- worth up to roughly
$12.2 billion if Google exercises the option in full [1][2]. The warrant was
issued 18 August and disclosed in a Form 8-K filed with the US Securities
and Exchange Commission; neither company has published its own press
release or made an on-the-record statement about the deal [1].

The warrant's shares vest as Google buys Marvell's "Custom Products": an
initial 1,360,867 shares vest in quarterly installments over the first
year, and the rest vests in up to 240 further tranches of $500 million in
qualifying revenue each, running through fiscal 2033 [1] -- implying as
much as $120 billion in cumulative revenue to Marvell if every tranche is
earned.

The warrant formalizes a commercial agreement the two companies signed on
29 July [1]. It covers silicon that attaches to Google's AI infrastructure
-- inference accelerators, storage controllers, network interface
controllers, memory interface controllers and near-memory compute --
rather than Google's Tensor Processing Unit chip itself, which continues to
be built primarily with Broadcom [1][5]. Morningstar analyst William Kerwin
told Reuters the deal is "a big win for Marvell" that represents "a growing
pie at Google for new sources, rather than a competitive displacement of
Broadcom" [3].

Marvell shares jumped on the news -- as much as roughly 14% in premarket
trading by one outlet's figures, paring back to high-single or low-double
digits through the session depending on the outlet and time cited -- while
Broadcom shares fell and Alphabet's were little changed [3][4][5].

## Why it matters

The deal gives Google a second custom-silicon supplier for AI-infrastructure
chips alongside Broadcom, its longstanding TPU partner, without displacing
that relationship -- analyst commentary in the coverage frames it as
expanding the overall pie rather than a switch [3]. For Marvell, tying
warrant vesting directly to Google's future purchases links its financial
upside to how much AI infrastructure Google actually buys in the years
ahead, not just to today's announcement.
