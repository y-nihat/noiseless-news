## What happened

Klaviyo announced on 5 August 2026 that it is acquiring the team and
technology of Agency, an AI-native customer-success startup founded in 2023
by Elias Torres, including Agency's software and related intellectual
property [1]. Neither company disclosed a purchase price, and the deal is
expected to close in the third quarter of 2026 [1][2]. Agency had raised $32
million from Sequoia, Menlo Ventures and Felicis before the deal [2].

Once the transaction closes, Torres will join Klaviyo as chief product
officer, leading Agency's 25-person team and Klaviyo's AI agent product line
-- Composer, which builds marketing campaigns, and Customer Agent, which
handles post-sale support such as returns and order tracking -- reporting to
co-CEO Andrew Bialecki [1][2]. The deal reunites the two: Torres hired
Bialecki as one of his first engineers at Performable, a company HubSpot
acquired in 2011, and Bialecki later invited Torres to angel-invest in
Klaviyo's 2015 seed round [1][2]. Torres also co-founded Drift, where he was
CTO for eight years until its $1.2 billion sale to Vista Equity in 2021 [2].

## Why it matters

The acquisition adds Torres's AI-agent team directly to Klaviyo's push to
embed autonomous agents into its marketing and customer-data platform, which
Klaviyo says stores more than nine billion consumer profiles [1]. It is a
personnel-and-technology deal rather than a product launch: Klaviyo is buying
an AI-agent-building team and folding it into existing products, not shipping
something new outright.
