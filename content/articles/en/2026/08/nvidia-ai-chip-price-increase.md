## What happened

Nvidia has told customers to expect price increases of more than 15% on AI
hardware built on its Grace Blackwell 300 and Vera Rubin 200 chip
generations, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter
[1]. The Information separately reported the increase at about 17%, citing
server makers who are passing the higher cost on to their own customers
[2] — a different attribution chain from Bloomberg's account of Nvidia
notifying customers directly, indicating two independently sourced reports
rather than one being relayed by the other. The new pricing applies to
systems shipping from early 2027, according to Bloomberg [1]. Nvidia did
not respond to Bloomberg's request for comment [1].

## Why it matters

A double-digit price increase across Nvidia's flagship AI chip platforms
raises the cost of building AI data centers at a moment when AI
infrastructure spending is already under scrutiny, for customers with
little practical alternative to Nvidia hardware at the frontier of AI
compute.
