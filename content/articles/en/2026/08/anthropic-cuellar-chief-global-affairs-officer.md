## What happened

Anthropic said on 4 August 2026 that Mariano-Florentino "Tino" Cuéllar is
joining the company as its first Chief Global Affairs Officer, a new role
covering policy, strategic international engagement and government
relationships worldwide [1]. He will report to Anthropic President Daniela
Amodei [1][2].

Cuéllar most recently served as president of the Carnegie Endowment for
International Peace and was previously a Justice of the Supreme Court of
California, where he wrote opinions on technology, privacy, international
agreements and separation-of-powers issues [1]. He has also directed
Stanford's Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies, co-directed
its Center for International Security and Cooperation, and served on the
President's Intelligence Advisory Board and the State Department's Foreign
Affairs Policy Board across three presidential administrations [1]. He will
take a leave of absence from his posts as a professor at Stanford Law School
and senior fellow at Stanford's Institute for Human-Centered Artificial
Intelligence [1][2]. Before this appointment, Cuéllar served as a trustee of
Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust [1].

## Why it matters

The hire creates a dedicated executive seat for Anthropic's dealings with
governments and international policymakers as the company navigates an
increasingly contentious regulatory landscape [2].
