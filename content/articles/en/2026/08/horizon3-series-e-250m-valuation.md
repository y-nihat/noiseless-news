## What happened

Horizon3, the maker of the autonomous penetration-testing platform
NodeZero, raised a $250 million Series E funding round at a valuation
above $2 billion, announced 3 August 2026 [1][2]. The round was co-led
by existing investors NightDragon and NEA, with participation from
Acrew Capital, Blue Cloud Ventures, Demeter Group, EDBI, PSG, SAIC,
Sapphire Ventures, Craft Ventures, Prosperity7 Ventures, Qualcomm
Ventures, Ridge Ventures, and SignalFire [1].

The new valuation is roughly triple the $650 million Horizon3 was
valued at in its Series D round about a year earlier [1][2]. The
company says annual recurring revenue grew 120% year-over-year, that
NodeZero has run more than 310,000 tests in production, and that it now
serves more than 7,000 customers globally [1][2].

Horizon3 says the new funds will go toward international expansion —
including Singapore, Australia, and deeper presence in EMEA — as well
as continued go-to-market scaling and development of autonomous
attack-and-defense capabilities [1][2].

## Why it matters

NodeZero belongs to a growing category of security tools that use
automation to actively exploit weaknesses, rather than just listing
them, to show organizations what an attacker could actually reach.
Horizon3's valuation tripling in about a year points to strong investor
appetite for that approach as AI lowers the cost of both mounting and
defending against automated attacks.
