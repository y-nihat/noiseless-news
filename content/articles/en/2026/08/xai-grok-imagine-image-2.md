## What happened

xAI shipped Grok Imagine Image 2.0 as the new "Quality Mode" of its image
tool, available on grok.com/imagine and in the Grok iOS and Android apps,
around 7-8 August 2026 [1][2]. The release adds region-based ("magic wand")
edits, subject segmentation, background removal with transparent export,
multi-reference editing that accepts up to five input images, layout-aware
resizing across aspect ratios, and preset workflow templates for tasks such
as product shots, headshots and game assets [1][2]. API access is not yet
available; xAI says it is "coming soon" but has not given a date or price
[1]. xAI's own description frames the model as improving fidelity across
photography, design and illustration work — a vendor characterization not
independently reproduced [1].

## Why it matters

The release adds editing-focused tools — regional edits, multi-image
reference, transparent-background export — to Grok's image product,
expanding it from generation into iterative editing workflows. With API
access still unavailable, the update is for now a consumer-app feature
rather than something developers can build on.
