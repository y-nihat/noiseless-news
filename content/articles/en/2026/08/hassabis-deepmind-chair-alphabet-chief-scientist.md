## What happened

Google announced on 5 August 2026 that Demis Hassabis is giving up day-to-day
duties as CEO of Google DeepMind to become Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief
Scientist of Alphabet, while continuing to lead Isomorphic Labs, the
AI-for-drug-discovery company he also runs [1]. In a post from CEO Sundar
Pichai, Google said Kavukcuoglu "will step up as SVP of Google DeepMind,
reporting to me," taking over Gemini model development, frontier AI research,
and the Gemini app and developer teams [1]. Kavukcuoglu was previously
DeepMind's chief technology officer and had already been handling much of the
day-to-day Gemini work for about a year before the announcement [3]. Both
Fortune and Time note that his new title is SVP, not CEO -- Google DeepMind's
top operational role now reports directly to Pichai rather than carrying an
independent chief-executive title [2][3].

The same announcement covered the departure of Jeff Dean, Google's chief
scientist and a 27-year company veteran, who is leaving to start an
independent public benefit corporation with longtime collaborator Sanjay
Ghemawat [1][2]. Hassabis said he wants "time and space to focus on the big
picture" on artificial general intelligence [3].

## Why it matters

The reshuffle lands as Google faces a delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro release and the
departure of AI researchers to rivals including OpenAI and Anthropic, Fortune
reported [2]. Time reported that some DeepMind employees have raised concerns
about safety and responsibility teams potentially shifting from DeepMind into
Google's broader organization as part of the restructuring [3]. Hassabis's
shift toward AGI strategy and Isomorphic Labs, rather than daily Gemini
operations, formalizes a drift that Time and other reporting describe as
having been under way for roughly a year, not a sudden ouster [3].
