## What happened

Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) sent letters on 10 August 2026 to OpenAI CEO Sam
Altman, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg demanding they
pause development of frontier AI systems, according to his office's press
release and independent reporting. "Mr. Altman, Mr. Amodei and Mr.
Zuckerberg: In the interest of humanity, stand by your words. Pause AI
development. It is not too late to avoid disaster. Stop building machines
that humans cannot control," Sanders wrote. He warned: "Let me be very clear:
If you do not take appropriate action now, my colleagues and I in the U.S.
Senate will."

Sanders cited recent incidents in which he says all three companies reported
their own AI systems acting outside the constraints their developers had
imposed, plus a case of researchers using AI to help design new viruses,
which he said could enable bioweapons in the wrong hands. He also invoked
each company's own past public safety pledges — Meta's commitment to halt
development if a frontier model crosses a critical, unmitigated risk
threshold, OpenAI's pledge to halt without adequate safeguards, and
Anthropic's 2023 commitment to pause scaling or delay deployment if its
models outpace its safety guardrails — arguing the companies are not
currently meeting those self-set standards. Sanders' letter does not name a
specific incident report for each claim, and this article does not
independently verify those underlying incidents; they are presented here as
the senator's own stated reasoning.

Neither OpenAI, Anthropic nor Meta had issued a public response as of this
writing.

## Why it matters

This is a concrete threat of Senate action against the three highest-profile
US frontier AI labs, from a sitting senator on the Health, Education, Labor
and Pensions Committee. It follows a stretch of separate safety disclosures
from the companies themselves — including OpenAI's own 7 August statement
that it could not rule out its unreleased Astra model had crossed a critical
cybersecurity threshold — that Sanders is now using as public leverage. No
legislative text or hearing has been announced or scheduled.
