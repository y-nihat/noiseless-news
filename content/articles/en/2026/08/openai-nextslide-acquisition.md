## What happened

NextSlide, a startup that built an app turning prompts, notes, documents and
research into polished, editable presentations, said on 8 August 2026 that it
has joined OpenAI [1]. Founder Ahmed Beshry said the deal closed earlier in
2026 but was only announced publicly now, and that the NextSlide team is now
working on ChatGPT [1][2]. Financial terms were not disclosed [2].

Beshry, who previously co-founded the cashier-less checkout startup Caper AI
(acquired by Instacart in 2021), said the team will keep pursuing its mission
of "building AI products that help people create, communicate, and turn their
ideas into meaningful work" inside OpenAI [2].

## Why it matters

OpenAI has acquired several small product teams this year to build
first-party ChatGPT features rather than developing them from scratch.
NextSlide's presentation-generation technology points toward more built-in
document and slide creation inside ChatGPT, though neither company has said
whether or when a NextSlide-derived feature will ship.
