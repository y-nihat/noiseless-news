## What happened

Meta released a native macOS desktop app for Meta AI on 19 August 2026,
adding to the existing meta.ai website and mobile app [1]. The app lets
users share a window during a session so Meta AI can give feedback on the
work on screen, and adds system-wide dictation across Mac apps [1].

Aimed at small businesses, the app connects Meta AI directly to a user's
Instagram and Facebook accounts for engagement analytics, to Meta
ad-campaign performance data for optimization suggestions, and to Google
Workspace — Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Slides [1]. Independent coverage from
The Verge and Engadget, published the same day, described the same
feature set [2][3].

## Why it matters

The connectors are the notable part: rather than a general-purpose
assistant, this is Meta AI positioned as a business tool with standing
access to a user's social accounts, ad data and Workspace documents. A Mac
app also extends Meta AI beyond the browser and phone into the desktop
workflow most small-business owners already use for that data.
