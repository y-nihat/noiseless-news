## What happened

Mistral AI said on 11 August 2026 that its Regional Endpoints feature,
which lets customers pin where their inference processing happens —
Europe or the US — is now generally available [1][2]. Alongside it, the
company launched Mistral Priority Tier in public preview, offering
mission-critical customers custom rate limits and a committed uptime
SLA; Mistral says it is the only European AI lab offering both regional
choice and an SLA-backed service tier [1].

Mistral also said a coalition of European enterprises — including
chipmaking-equipment maker ASML, shipping group CMA CGM, travel-tech
firm Amadeus, and French state investment bank Caisse des Dépôts — has
made multi-year compute-capacity commitments. Mistral says these
commitments underwrite 200 megawatts of European compute infrastructure
by the end of 2027, scaling to a full gigawatt by the end of 2030 [1][2].
Separately, Mistral's platform now supports third-party open-weight
models alongside its own, starting with Z.ai's GLM-5.2 [1].

## Why it matters

The announcement bundles product moves (regional data residency, SLA
tiers) with a longer-term infrastructure pledge, positioning Mistral as
a European alternative to US hyperscaler-hosted inference as sovereign-AI
procurement rules tighten across the EU. The capacity figures are
Mistral's own multi-year targets, not yet-built infrastructure.
