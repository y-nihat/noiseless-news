## What happened

Databricks said on 16 July 2026 that it has signed a term sheet for a
strategic funding round valuing the data-analytics and AI platform company
at $188 billion, led by existing investor Coatue Management and expected to
close later this summer [1][2]. The company has not disclosed how much
money the round will raise; Wall Street Journal reporting, citing people
familiar with the matter, put the investment at $3 billion, a figure
Databricks itself has not confirmed [2][3].

The new valuation is roughly a 40% increase from the $134 billion valuation
Databricks reached in February 2026, when it raised about $5 billion [2][3].
Databricks said the capital will go toward expanding three AI products —
the Unity AI Gateway (multi-model governance), the Genie AI assistant, and
the Lakebase serverless database for AI agents — as well as potential
future AI acquisitions [1].

## Why it matters

Two funding rounds in five months, each larger than the last, show investor
demand for AI infrastructure platforms remains strong even as some AI
spending is drawing scrutiny elsewhere in the market. The gap between what
Databricks has confirmed (the valuation, the lead investor, the intended use
of funds) and what remains sourced only to an unconfirmed leak (the exact
$3 billion check size) is typical of how these announcement-before-close
deals get reported.
