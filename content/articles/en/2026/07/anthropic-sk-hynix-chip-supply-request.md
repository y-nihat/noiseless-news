## What happened

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei said at a San Francisco summit on 24 July 2026, tied to South Korean President Lee Jae-myung's visit, that Anthropic has signed supply agreements with both Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix [1][2][3]. Amodei did not disclose which components the deals cover, their value, or how long they run [1][2]. The remarks came alongside separate, larger memory-supply agreements — reported at a combined value near $950 billion — that Samsung and SK Hynix struck with Nvidia, Microsoft and Broadcom at the same event [3].

This follows an earlier, narrower report: on 25 July 2026, Bloomberg reported SK Group chairman Chey Tae-won saying, at a different San Francisco event, that Anthropic had asked SK Hynix for chip-manufacturing supplies to build its own custom semiconductors [4]. That report traced to one source — the chairman's on-stage remarks as relayed by a single wire story — and named only SK Hynix, describing a request rather than a signed deal. Amodei's own on-record statement, since independently reported by multiple outlets, both confirms the SK Hynix relationship has moved to a signed agreement and adds Samsung as a second named partner.

## Why it matters

A named executive's on-record statement is stronger evidence than a third party's relayed account, which is why this clears a verification bar the earlier chairman's-quote report did not. Because no chip type, value, or timeline was disclosed, the size and shape of Anthropic's manufacturing commitment to either company remains an open question.
