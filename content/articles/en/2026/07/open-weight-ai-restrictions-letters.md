## What happened

Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, and 22 other companies — including Hugging Face, IBM, Dell, Palantir, Mozilla, Mistral, Y Combinator, and the Linux Foundation — published a joint statement on 24 July 2026 titled "Open Weights and American AI Leadership," opposing what they call premature restrictions on open-weight AI models [1]. The statement, hosted on Microsoft's site and amplified by Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, argues that "the right response to this risk is not to prohibit open weights" and warns against conflating legitimate model-distillation techniques with IP misappropriation [1]. The letter lands as Washington weighs how to respond to fast-improving Chinese open-weight models such as Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 [2][3]. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are notably absent from the 25 signatories [3].

Two days earlier, a separate coalition acted on a narrower question. Nearly 200 US startups, organized under the newly formed Little Tech Association, sent letters to President Trump, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, and other administration officials, obtained by Politico, urging them not to cut off American builders' access to downloading Chinese open-weight models [5]. That letter argues for "a scalpel rather than a sledgehammer" and warns that a blanket download ban would not stop the models' proliferation but would weaken US startups that already build on them [5]. Y Combinator is among the signatories of both efforts.

## Why it matters

The two letters, filed within 48 hours of each other, show a split emerging in how the US AI industry wants Washington to handle Chinese open-weight competition: major model and infrastructure companies are lobbying against restricting open-weight models as a category, while a separate bloc of smaller startups is focused specifically on preserving access to Chinese open-weight downloads. The absence of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google from the broader letter marks a visible split between companies that sell mostly closed frontier models and those with more to gain from an open-weight ecosystem remaining unrestricted.
