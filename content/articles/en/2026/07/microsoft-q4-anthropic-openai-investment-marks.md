## What happened

Microsoft's fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 earnings release, published 29 July
2026, disclosed that the company recorded a $3.2 billion mark-to-market gain
on its equity investment in Anthropic during the quarter ended 30 June 2026
[1]. The same release disclosed a separate $480 million net gain on
Microsoft's OpenAI investment for the quarter, adding $0.07 to diluted
earnings per share, and a $4.96 billion gain from OpenAI for the full fiscal
year, adding $0.67 [1].

Microsoft's release groups the Anthropic gain with two other discrete items
— lower-than-expected costs from its Voluntary Retirement Program, partly
offset by Xbox severance and impairment charges — as jointly contributing
$0.27 to diluted EPS for the quarter [1]. That $0.27 is a combined figure
for all of those items together, not the Anthropic gain in isolation;
TechCrunch's report on the same release described the Anthropic gain alone
as adding "33 cents" to EPS, a figure not supported by Microsoft's own
wording [1][2]. Overall, Microsoft reported $90.0 billion in quarterly
revenue, up 18% year over year, and $35.8 billion in GAAP net income, with
Azure and other cloud services revenue up 43% [1][3].

## Why it matters

The disclosure gives a rare, audited look at how Microsoft's minority stakes
in the two leading US AI labs are actually performing on its books: a single
quarter of Anthropic mark-to-market gains ($3.2 billion) came close to
matching a full year of OpenAI gains ($4.96 billion), even though Microsoft's
OpenAI position is by far the larger and longer-held of the two. It is also
a reminder that headline EPS figures for AI investment marks often bundle
multiple unrelated items — reading the underlying earnings release, not just
the framing of any one gain, is necessary to isolate what a specific
investment actually contributed.
